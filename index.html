About the Correspondent
E. C. Eckman IV, Correspondent — photographed at home.

Recently, the company I was working for asked me to get up and move to some cold, mountainless state… or find a new job.

I said no.

And suddenly I have all of the time I could want to develop some powerful technical skills I’ve had my eyes on—and also just to have a whole lot of fun. A step on that journey is making my own website, getting to know a good bit of web-dev in the process.

I will be adding features to the site fairly often, allowing myself to publish things that are ugly… until I know how to make them pretty. After leaning on my buddy Claude AI for design, though all of the text is mine, this is what we’ve come up with.

Thanks for being here!
