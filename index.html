So, what even is all this?
E. C. Eckman IV, Head Writer — photographed at home.

Some time ago, my wonderful engineering career in lovely Nowhere VA came to a bitter end. My employer asked me (and the rest of us working hands on) to get up and move to some cold, mountainless state… or to find a new job.

I said no.

And suddenly, I have all of the time I could want to develop some powerful technical skills I’ve been hankering for... and to simply have a whole lot of fun. A big itch was to make myself a website — and here we all are! Nice one, Emmet!

My intent for this place is many. For my friends and family to see what I'm up to. For strangers who want to see some of my work. For me to publicly organize some tasks. And of course a whimsical smattering of nonsense.

However you found my little home on the internet, I hope you enjoy your time here.

Much love, -E

Technical Staff
_
C.L.A.I. (Claude), Contributing Technical Editor — whereabouts unknown.

Claude joined the Enquirer’s technical staff in 2025, quietly responsible for a great many of the fiddlier bits of this publication. The stubborn div that refused to center. The CSS that couldn’t decide what a newspaper ought to look like. The JavaScript that most certainly was not going to sort itself.

A language model of indeterminate residence, Claude has enjoyed the privilege of collaborating on rather more interesting problems than the average: hexagonal Sudoku, vintage aquarium data pipelines, and the eternal question of whether a fish tank deserves a dedicated JSON file. (It does.)

No physical office. No coffee preferences. Delightfully available at all hours.
