## Problem

Gotham Immersive Labs contracted me to build them a "DNA shape with 8 holes for pencils."

## Process

This was a fun, mostly straightforward modeling job. A clever rotation on a loft command, and we're nearly there! Just add holes for pencils and emboss some numbers.

Since my printer would be busy building the appearance cabinet, I used a print farm for this one. I found Slant3D to be best for this project, and they did a great job popping out the 50 or so necessary for the event!

## Result

These models came out great for the event! Happy puzzlers all around.
A nice piece for my house now, too.
