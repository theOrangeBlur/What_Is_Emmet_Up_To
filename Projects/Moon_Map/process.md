## Problem
Have a moving representation of where the moon is directly overhead on the Earth's surface in real time!
I've been wanting to work on this for a while.

## Process
Idea 1:
2 axis gimble that holds the moon in the center
Idea 2: 
1 axis standard gimble (worm gear?), height controlled by a winch.
This can be fixed up to look like a hand dangling the moon by a string!
Will the changing radius of the winch make it hard to control height precicely?  --Consider using a "capstan winch!"
