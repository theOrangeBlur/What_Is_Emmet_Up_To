Daily Snapshots

A visual, somewhat random smattering of my life

^^ About This ^^

This video is compiled from photos and videos I've taken, inspired by the "One Second Every Day" concept. Each time I add new media, the compilation updates automatically.

Having lost all of my digital pictures before 2025, I have started to capture more of my life digitally and treasure those glimpses a little bit more. After leaving my job in October, I also started taking short videos explicitly for this purpose.
