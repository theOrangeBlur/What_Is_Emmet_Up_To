^^ About This ^^

This video is compiled from photos and videos I've taken, inspired by the "One Second Every Day" concept. I periodically move some of my Google photos into a Google Drive folder that gets scraped by a github action.

Having lost all of my digital pictures before 2025, I have started to capture more of my life in photos and treasure those glimpses a little bit more. After leaving my job in October, I also started taking short videos explicitly for this purpose.
