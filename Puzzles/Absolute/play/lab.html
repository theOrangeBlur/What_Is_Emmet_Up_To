Rules

  • Fill some thermometers starting from the bulb, with no gaps. A thermometer can be partly filled or empty.
  • Each thermometer is filled with red or blue, never both.
  • A red number counts the red cells in its row or column, a blue number the blue cells, and a black number all filled cells. Lines with no number have no constraint.
  • Black clues are never 0. An empty line shows a red 0, a blue 0, or no clue.
  • Every generated puzzle has exactly one solution.
  • Mark thermo (or Ctrl-click) tints a thermometer pale red or blue when you know its color but not its level. Emptying it keeps the mark, and filling it uses the marked color.
  • Cell marks: ✕ means empty, ✕ means not red, ✕ means not blue. A color ✕ on a bulb marks the whole thermometer the other color instead. Hint takes all of them into account.
  • While solving, the gray Either fill marks cells you know are filled before you know their color. They count toward black clues only.

Difficulty tiers

  1. Saturation: a clue equals the most or least its line can hold.
  2. Line bounds: counting what the other thermometers can still add.
  3. Line combinations: only some exact combinations of levels add up.
  4. Crossing lines: two lines that share thermometers, looked at together.
  5. Contradiction: assume a cell, follow the counts, and find a broken clue.

Tiers 1–2 are counting: reasoning along one row or column. Tiers 3–5 are jumps, where counting is stuck and you have to think harder. A jump's chain is how many lines you hold at once: 2 for crossing lines, and for a contradiction the lines you follow from the assumption to the broken clue (3 or more).

Easy = no jumps. Medium = 1–2 jumps, chains ≤ 2 (no contradictions). Hard = up to 4 jumps, chains ≤ 4. Expert = more than that (the preset caps chains at 6). Medium and up avoid clues within 1 of 0 or full. "Needs guessing" means the logic solver got stuck.
