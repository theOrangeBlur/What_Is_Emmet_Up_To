How to play
  • Fill some thermometers from the bulb up, with no gaps. A thermometer can be partly filled or empty.
  • Each thermometer is red or blue, never both.
  • A red number counts the red cells in its row or column, a blue number the blue cells, and a black number all filled cells. A line with no number can be anything.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and it can be reached by logic alone.

Left-click fills red up to the cell, right-click fills blue, and clicking the top filled cell again empties it. Shift-click or drag marks ✕ (empty). Alt-click or Either fills gray: filled, color unknown. Ctrl-click or Mark thermo tints a thermometer's color without filling it. The red ✕ and blue ✕ tools mark "not red" and "not blue".

On a phone, pick a tool and tap. Keys: 1–6 pick tools, W toggles Mark thermo, H hints, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo.

Daily rules: each hint adds 30 seconds to your time. Revealing the solution ends the run without a score.
